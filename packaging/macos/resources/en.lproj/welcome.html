This installs claudia composes, claudia computes and claudia creates for everyone on this Mac, and uninstall claudia in Applications ▸ Utilities.

Each person's documents stay on this Mac, in her own place, "Private on this computer".

Under Customize you can also install the claudia service: a shared place, "Shared on this computer", for the people of this Mac to share documents. It is not installed unless you choose it.

claudia's apps that are open, also those of other people of this Mac, are closed while it is installed.

claudia needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or newer.
