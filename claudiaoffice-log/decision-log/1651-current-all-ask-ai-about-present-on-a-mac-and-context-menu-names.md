# D1651 · Ask ai… is one action in Tools and Help; About says About; Present has letter keys on a Mac; the names of the context menus

**Tools ▸ Ask ai… and Help ▸ Ask ai… are one action, `tools.ask` on Ctrl+F1, shown in both menus, each place with its own rule. Help ▸ About reads "About", as the workbook says. On macOS, Tools ▸ Present ▸ From Slide is Ctrl+Shift+Option+H and From Beginning Ctrl+Option+P, the letter keys of D344, because F5 and Shift+F5 are the system's; elsewhere they stay Shift+F5 and F5. Preferences ▸ Shortcuts names the context menus Object, Cell, Paragraph, List, Table, Heading, Sheet Tab or Slide Tab, Slide, Row Header, Column Header, Chart on a Sheet, Pivot Table, Reference.**

**Notes.** The owner's answers to Q1–Q3 and Q5 after slice 2 of the menu work. The workbook's macOS column for the two Present rows changed with this decision.
