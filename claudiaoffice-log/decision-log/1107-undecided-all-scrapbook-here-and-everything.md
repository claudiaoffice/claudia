# D1107 · The scrapbook shows what belongs here first, and everything second, in quiet one-line rows

**Proposal: View ▸ Scrapbook is a dock on the left with two views. "Here" lists the items linked to the chapter, named range or slide the person is in; "Everything" lists all of them, as folders or by place.** Items are one-line rows (kind icon, title, a ring showing in how many places the item sits, an open-point flag). Only the selected item opens into a detail card with its full text, its places, "＋ Place", "Open point" and "@ai". Items not yet sorted are one slim count row (the inbox), never a list that interrupts. Like the History & Chat dock (D40) the scrapbook has a hide/show arrow on its edge, and both docks can be hidden to the sides. In creates the scrapbook is a second tab next to the slide strip.

**Notes.** Undecided: the owner found the first scrapbook too heavy, chose this reduced look, and wants to think about it further before it is built. Own folders: one folder per item, plus automatic Inbox, Open points, Sources and Media.
