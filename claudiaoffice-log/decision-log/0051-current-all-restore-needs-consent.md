# D51 · Restoring a checkpoint in a live session needs the consent of everybody who is editing

**Restoring a checkpoint in a live session needs the consent of everybody who is editing.** Any editing user may propose going back to checkpoint X; all other editing users get a banner asking for consent; only if all agree is it restored, and the old history stays on the hidden side line (D48). Someone who is not present is not asked. A **later feature** lets users see the lines and pick one.

**Notes.** Refines D48. The kernel can already restore, list lines and switch; the consent flow and wire methods are E3.7, the line picker E3.8. Details: D52.
