# D1102 · Focus mode hides the surroundings, and holds the AI and the chat back instead of losing them

**View ▸ Focus mode hides the header, panels, toolbar and status bar of the app; it also makes the AI and the chat quiet.** While quiet, AI suggestions and @-mentions are held, not dropped: a small pill shows how many are held, and leaving focus mode (or clicking the pill) shows them in order. **Tools ▸ Quiet ai and chat** does the same without hiding any panel. Nothing is lost, nobody is told that someone else is in focus mode, and the AI stays one user among others (the one ai user per document stays as it is).

**Notes.** No timer and no "you have been focused for…" message (D1101). Messages addressed to a person in quiet mode are delivered into the history and chat as usual; only the popping-up is held. Mocks: screens 02 and 12 of the workflow mock round.
