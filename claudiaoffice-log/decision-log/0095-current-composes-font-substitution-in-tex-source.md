# D95 · Font substitution is applied by `claudia-typeset` in the TeX source, from `Catalogue.resolve`, not by fontconfig

**Font substitution is applied by `claudia-typeset` in the TeX source, from `Catalogue.resolve`, not by fontconfig.**

**Notes.** *Finding, not a choice:* XeTeX with fontspec ignores fontconfig `<alias>` and `<match>/<edit>`.
