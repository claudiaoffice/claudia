# D1619 · A right press on a paragraph or on a slide opens a context menu

**A right press on a paragraph in composes (or in a text box) opens its context menu: Name & describe…, Style…, Reference in chat; Style… opens the same popover as Format ▸ Style ▸ Apply…, which a right press used to open directly. A right press on a slide in creates opens the slide's context menu, the same as its tab's: Name & describe…, Reference in chat.**

**Notes.** One pattern for every thing: the right press gives the thing's menu, and its first entries name and style it. Styling a paragraph takes one press more than before. The slide had no menu, which D1603 forbids (a right press never looks broken).
