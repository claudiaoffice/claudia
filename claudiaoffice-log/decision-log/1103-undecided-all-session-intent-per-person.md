# D1103 · A session intent is a private line per person, not a command in the document

**Proposal: a person may state, at the start of a session, what she wants to do now, in a line of her own.** It is optional, can be skipped and switched off, and belongs to the person, not to the document: it is not a logged command and others do not see it. It may link to the document's goal if D71's metadata exists. The AI may read it only if the person asks it to (D1114).

**Notes.** Undecided until the owner has tried the mock. The reason it is not a command is the same as for the private drawer (D1110): a plan for one's own evening is not part of the document's history.
