# D1007 · Purpose tints print only when asked

**The tints that show what cells are for (input, calculation, result) print when *Show what cells are for* is on in Sheet options. It is off by default.** A cell that has a fill of its own keeps it.

**Notes.** A printout for a boss or a bank should not be coloured by our idea of what a cell is for, a printout for a class should be. The legend is not printed: the colours are the three of D200's roles, and an option that exists only for teaching can add a legend later.
