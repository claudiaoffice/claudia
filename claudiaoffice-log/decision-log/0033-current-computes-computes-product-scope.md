# D33 · claudia computes

**claudia computes**: classical Excel-like functions with more elegance, user-friendliness and hidden power. Fields: project management, financial modelling, sciences, data analysis and interactive visualization, education and simulation. Inspired by Bret Victor.

**Notes.** The Bret Victor features (sliders, what-if, dependency views) are not built.

**Checked against the code, 2026-11-17 (`main` at `6814bac`).** Two of the three are built since: what-if as alternatives and their outcomes (E6.21, D294–D296) and a dependency view as Follow the computation (E6.23, D297). Sliders bound to cells are not (E6.10).
