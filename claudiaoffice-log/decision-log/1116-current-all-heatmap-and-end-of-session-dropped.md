# D1116 · The edit heatmap and an end-of-session reflection are not part of the workflow for now

**The heatmap of where a document was edited, and a reflection screen at the end of a session, are dropped.** The corkboard gives the overview and the progress picture (D1105), and the session timeline looks back (D1104).

**Notes.** Dropped because they measure the person's activity rather than help her work (D1101). They may be reconsidered if people ask for them.
