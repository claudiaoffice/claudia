# D1114 · The AI reads a private item only when its owner asks, and answers stay private

**The AI sees a person's private notes only while that person is the one asking (D261), and what it writes about a private item stays in the private notes.** It cannot read one person's private notes on another's behalf, and it does not use private items for suggestions to other users.

**Notes.** The AI is one user among others (the one ai user per document) and cannot hold keys of its own to anyone's private notes.
