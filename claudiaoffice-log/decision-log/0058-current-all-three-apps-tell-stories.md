# D58 · The three apps work together to tell stories in different formats

**The three apps work together to tell stories in different formats.** A spreadsheet that simulates something, a text that explains the background, a presentation that summarises the results and tells the arc from inspiration to insight. Moving insights between the apps must be truly easy. Users are not pushed into specific ways of thinking. The story is told and developed with critical thinking.
