# D89 · `claudia-fonts` and `claudia-typeset` are packages outside the stdlib-only kernel

**`claudia-fonts` and `claudia-typeset` are packages outside the stdlib-only kernel.** `claudia-typeset` depends on `claudia-fonts`; both are under the API snapshot rule.
