# D1606 · The trial: every reason starts at hide, switched in a developer setting, with an override per entry

**For the trial, whether a reason hides or greys is a developer setting, not a choice in the app: a file beside the client's other settings, and a URL parameter for the web. Every reason starts at hide. An entry can be set on its own to always show, grey or hide, and its own setting wins over its reason's.** Whether people get the choice is decided after the trial. **The menu rebuild (one menu definition for every client) carries the rules from its first build**, so the menus are rebuilt once. **The ai's actions are unaffected**: what a menu shows is about the person's view; the ai acts within the rights of the person who asks (D261–D263) and its action catalog (E7.12) stays complete.

**Notes.** The override per entry answers the owner's question on the trial: an entry such as Undo can be kept visible without a rule that no entry ever hides.
