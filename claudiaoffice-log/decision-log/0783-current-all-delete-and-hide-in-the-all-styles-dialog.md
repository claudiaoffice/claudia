# D783 · Delete and hide in the All styles dialog: the screens

**Built** (web client; the shells open the page's own dialog, D301/D786). In All styles (D302) each row gets *Delete…* (document styles) or *Hide* (built-in and template ones, which show *hidden* and *Show* once hidden; *Show hidden styles* lists them again). *Delete…* opens a confirmation that says in words what will happen — which places have the style, which styles are based on it — and offers two ways in this order (D785): *Assign another style* (the default, with the plain style of the kind preselected; says in amber what will look different, and how many places) and *Re-base on another style* (explained, with the styles that cannot hold the look listed with the reason, not offered). After re-basing the stand-in "<name> (kept)" is announced in the dialog with *Name it…* and *Undo*. Mock: `notes/mockups/style-delete-and-hide.svg` (approved by the owner on 2026-11-18). The same dialog serves all three apps; the labels go through the catalogs.
