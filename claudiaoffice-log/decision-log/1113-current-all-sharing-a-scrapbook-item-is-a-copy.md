# D1113 · Sharing a scrapbook item makes a copy; the private original stays private

**Sharing an item copies it into the shared document as an ordinary shared item: it appears in the history, can be redacted, shows its author, and is independent from then on.** The private original stays in the drawer with a "shared on …" mark that only its author sees. Nothing in the shared copy points back to the original.

**Notes.** A copy, not a link, because a person may afterwards change the note into something she would rather keep to herself again.
