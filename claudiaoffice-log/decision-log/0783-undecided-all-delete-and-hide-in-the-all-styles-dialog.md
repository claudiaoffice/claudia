# D783 · Delete and hide in the All styles dialog: the screens

**Proposal, not decided.** In All styles (D302) each row gets *Delete…* (document styles) or *Hide* (built-in and template ones, which show *Hidden* with *Show* once hidden). *Delete…* opens a confirmation that says in words what will happen — how many places have the style, which styles are based on it, "everything keeps its look" — and offers *Keep their look* (the default) or *Re-base on* a list of styles of the same kind, those that cannot hold the look greyed with the reason. After it a stand-in "<name> (kept)" is announced with an invitation to name it. Mock: `notes/mockups/style-delete-and-hide.svg`. The same dialog and the same menu entries in all three apps and both desktop shells; every entry gets a shortcut and the labels go through the catalogs.

Waiting for the owner's approval of the mock before any client code.
