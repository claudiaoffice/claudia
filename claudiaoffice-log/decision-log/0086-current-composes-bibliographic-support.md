# D86 · Composes has bibliographic support

**Composes has bibliographic support.** A **plugin** communicates with reference manager applications, for example Zotero or Mendeley. **The internal representation of bibliographies is BibTeX.**

**Notes.** BibTeX has no formal standard. Entry types, fields and text storage are open. Not started.
