# D916 · The print preview gets a Language choice and wraps its bar; the style editor gets Hyphenate — designed without a review

**The print preview's bar has a Language choice between Margins and Page numbers, with short names ("German", "English (GB)", "English (US)"), and the bar wraps onto a second line in a narrow window instead of scrolling, so Print… is always in sight. Notes above the pages say what is not hyphenated and what part of a picture is left out ("A picture is printed without some of it: filters (blurs, shadows) are left out."). The style editor's Paragraph section has a Hyphenate box under the alignment, for every app's styles.** The owner could not review mock-ups this time and asked Claude to choose the design; it is recorded as screenshots of the built screens (`notes/mockups/print-preview-language.png`, `notes/mockups/style-hyphenate.png`) to be looked at later.

**Notes.** Refines D906 (the preview's bar). The flow view shows nothing of hyphenation (D912).
