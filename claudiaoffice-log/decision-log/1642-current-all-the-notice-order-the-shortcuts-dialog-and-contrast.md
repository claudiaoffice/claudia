# D1642 · Which reason a notice names, the Shortcuts dialog's switch and tags, and the contrast of what says why

**When several conditions of an entry fail, its notice (D1604) and its line in an empty menu (D1603) name the first, in the order role, server, client, ai, document, selection. Preferences ▸ Shortcuts has a switch, "What Fits Now" and "Everything"; under Everything an action that does not fit is muted and tagged "not for you" (role), "not now" (document, selection) or "not here" (server, client, ai). Text that says why, the line of an empty menu and the muted rows of the Shortcuts dialog, is drawn in the muted ink (about 5:1 on white), not in the paler grey of a greyed entry.**

**Notes.** Who a person is matters more than what is selected: selecting something does not help a viewer. The paler grey is allowed only for controls that cannot be used (WCAG 1.4.3, BITV); text that carries information needs 4.5:1. The mock-ups are notes/mockups/menus-fit-*.svg.
