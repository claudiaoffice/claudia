# D71 · Goal, audience and stage are only suggestions and at most document metadata

**Goal, audience and stage are only suggestions and at most document metadata.** A user does not have to follow suggested phases or always have a goal. One option is offered: **"What would you like to achieve with this document?"**

**Notes.** Replaces the mandatory-brief proposal. The metadata belongs in the encrypted content domain, not the plain manifest, because a goal can be sensitive.
