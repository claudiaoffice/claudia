# D1106 · What a corkboard card is in each app, and the default status words

**composes: a card is a chapter or section. computes: a card is a named range, arranged by dependency or by sheet, and cards are not dragged (there is no order to change; the board is for overview and progress). creates: a card is a slide, carries its planned time and shows the total (D343).** Default status words, editable per document: composes Draft / Writing / Polishing; computes Draft / Building / Checked; creates Draft / Building / Polishing. The menu entry and its key are the same in all three apps.

**Notes.** Named ranges are unique names in the document (D550), which is what makes them stable cards. In computes the board is the experimental one: its use is overview, not reordering.
