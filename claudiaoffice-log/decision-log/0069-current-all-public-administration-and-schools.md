# D69 · Claudia is also for public administration and schools,

**Claudia is also for public administration and schools,** besides scientific users. The project wants to contribute to an open-source push for public organisations.

**Notes.** Consequences: open formats first, accessibility, plain-language help, official templates, self-hosting. The accessibility half is the weakest part of the product today (UX-01 to UX-05).
