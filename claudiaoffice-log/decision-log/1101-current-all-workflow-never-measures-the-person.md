# D1101 · Workflow support is optional, per person, and never measures the person

**Everything in the workflow feature is optional, belongs to the person using it, and never measures her.** No timers, scores, streaks, goals met or comparisons between people appear anywhere in it; nothing in it is a gate for another feature; a document in which nobody uses it behaves exactly as today. Status words, notes and flags are what people write themselves, not something the program counts.

**Notes.** Same ground rule as D71 for goal, audience and stage: help the person think, never steer or grade. It is what lets the focus mode, the corkboard and the binder be offered to everyone without becoming surveillance of work habits.
