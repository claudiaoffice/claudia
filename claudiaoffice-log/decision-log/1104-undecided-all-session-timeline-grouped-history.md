# D1104 · The session timeline is the history grouped by session, private to the viewer

**Proposal: History & Chat gets a "Group by session" toggle that folds the history into the person's own working sessions, with a 7-day strip.** It is a view over the history that already exists, shown only to the person who looks at it. It shows what was done, never how well; no scores (D1101). The History & Chat dock keeps exactly its two tabs (History & Chat, Help).

**Notes.** Undecided until the owner has tried the mock. The "end of session reflection" that was considered alongside it is not built (D1116).
