# D1654 · What only the page knows about an entry: not now, its choices, and what a key does

**The web's menus are the definition's (D1644), judged by the workbook's rules (D1646). Where a workspace knows that an entry cannot run now for a reason the workbook has no condition for (no history to replay, no reference at the caret, a shape this server does not offer, Cut and Copy of an object that is not a picture), the entry is greyed with the workspace's own sentence, never left out, and runs nothing. Where the workbook asks for choices the definition cannot hold (Wrap Text… ▸ its modes and Gap…), the web opens them; the key and a native shell still run the entry. A key of an entry that does not fit now, left out or greyed, says why (D1604), also Undo and Redo with nothing to take back. A submenu whose entries are all greyed is greyed itself. A menu with nothing left says "Nothing to insert: …" in English and keeps the sentence's capital in a language that writes on after a colon with one. Format ▸ Style ▸ Apply… and Edit… fit a new condition, "selection: something, or the cursor in a text": a style is for the paragraph the cursor is in.**

**Notes.** Made while building slice 4; each use of "not now" is listed in `implementation-notes/menus.md` for the owner, who may turn any of them into a condition of the workbook. Entries the web has and the workbook does not list (a reference's Show As and Remove Reference; computes' purpose of a table's column) stay beside the definition's for now: UX-99.
