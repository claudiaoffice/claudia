# D1109 · An open point is a binder item with a flag, and the tracker is the binder

**Proposal: there is no separate open-points list. A binder item (or a pinned place) can be flagged as an open point; resolving it keeps it, with an optional note.** F8 and Shift+F8 go to the next and previous open point. Markers in the margin, cells or objects: filled for open, outlined with a check for resolved, a ring for pinned only. An open point that is shared (D1113) may be exported as a comment to ODF and Office files; a private one is never exported.

**Notes.** Undecided together with the binder (D1107).
