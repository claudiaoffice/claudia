# D1610 · Built-in style and shape names are given in the language of the person who makes the document

**The built-in styles (Title, Heading 1, …) and shapes (Circle, …) are document data and keep the names they are given; they are named in the language of the person who makes the document, when it is made. Existing documents keep their names.**

**Notes.** Found by the menu audit: they show in English in a German menu. Work outside the menu work, story E5.25.
