# D1609 · Menu entries are written in header capitalisation, as the GNOME guidelines ask

**Menu entries and context-menu entries use header capitalisation ("Rename Sheet…", "Paste As"), as the GNOME Human Interface Guidelines ask for menu items. The workbook's words count, its capitalisation does not.**

**Notes.** The workbook was written in sentence case; the apps already follow the guidelines (the manifest's labels are "in the capitalisation of the GNOME HIG"), and Claudia is meant to be recognisable as a GNOME app (D3). The 31 rows of the Diagnosis sheet that differ only in capitalisation are therefore not work.
