# D1650 · A notice speaks per condition; an entry may have a sentence of its own

**The notice of an entry that does not fit (D1604), and the line of an empty menu (D1603), is the sentence of its first failing condition (D1642), one sentence per condition, such as "Only an owner can do this." The definition may give an entry a sentence of its own where it reads much better: Copy ("Only editors and owners can copy from this document.", D1640) and Share… ("Only an owner can share this document."). The condition "role: editor or owner" keeps today's sentence, "You can only look at this document."**

**Notes.** One condition covers many entries (Share… and everything under Manage ▸ for "role: owner"), so a sentence per entry would be dozens of texts to translate for little gain. The scenarios are `specs/menus/menus-that-fit.feature`.
