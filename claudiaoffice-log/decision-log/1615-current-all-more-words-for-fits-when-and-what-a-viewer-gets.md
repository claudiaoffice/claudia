# D1615 · More words for "fits when", and what a viewer gets

**D1601's list grows by: something selected · something to undo · something to redo · has something to refer to · a list item · has random numbers · a pivot table · a place to go back to · has objects; and for context menus: an object that holds text · an object that can turn · an object on the grid · more than one cell of the table · a merged cell. A viewer gets History & chat (the panel says it is for editors), Ask ai (the ai acts with her rights, D261, so it can only read and answer), but not Who wrote what or Replay… (D1261), nor All styles…, Palette or All numbering…; How do I and Documentation do not fit until there is help (E13).**

**Notes.** The words came from the first fill of the workbook's "Fits when" column (D1605).
