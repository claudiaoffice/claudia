# D1112 · Leaving the document, or being removed, deletes the person's private notes, and the dialogs say so

**When a person leaves (D144) or is removed, her private notes are deleted with her slot.** The leave dialog says in words that her private notes will be deleted and offers to export her own notes first. The owner's remove-user dialog says "any private notes of this person are deleted" without revealing whether she has any.

**Notes.** Private notes nobody can open would otherwise stay in the file for ever, and keeping one readable for the owner is excluded by D1111.
