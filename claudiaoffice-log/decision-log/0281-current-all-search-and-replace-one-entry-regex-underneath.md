# D281 · Search & replace is Edit ▸ Search & replace… in all three apps, regular expressions underneath, computes first

**Search & replace is Edit ▸ Search & replace… in all three apps**, the same entry in the same place. It is **built on regular expressions**, which the interface does not name: a person builds the pattern from a UI that **shows the effect** of what she builds, and the most important queries ("this text must not occur, but at least one of these characters") need no syntax. A person who knows regular expressions can **enter her own pattern as text** and apply it directly; that option exists but is not on the surface, as the CSS view of a style is (D73). It is **done in computes first** and searches **text in cells, formula expressions and values**; composes and creates follow with the same dialog.

**Notes.** The owner's brief of 2026-11-13, so `current`. What is not in the brief — the panel, the builder's vocabulary, the engine, the replace rules — is proposed in D282–D287 and is `undecided` until the owner has agreed the screens (`notes/search-and-replace.md`, `notes/mockups/`). Work is on branch `regex-builder`; this branch has the number range handed out in `ranges.md`.
