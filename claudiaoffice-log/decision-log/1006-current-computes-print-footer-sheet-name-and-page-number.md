# D1006 · A sheet's footer holds its name at the left and the page number at the right

**In the bottom margin, in the light 9 pt of D918: the sheet's name at the left margin, "n / N" at the right. The bar's Page numbers box switches the number; the sheet name has its own box in Sheet options (on by default). With Whole workbook the numbers run through the whole print and the name is each page's own sheet.**

**Notes.** One step beyond D904, whose footer is a centred page number; composes and creates keep theirs until E9.14 decides one footer layout for all three apps. Left to E9.14: headers.
