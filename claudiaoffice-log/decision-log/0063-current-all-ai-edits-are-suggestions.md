# D63 · Edits by the AI are suggestions

**Edits by the AI are suggestions** that a person reviews. People's contributions in live collaboration are **immediate**. Each contribution should be identifiable by the user it came from.

**Notes.** Built in composes (`suggest`, `accept_suggestion`, `reject_suggestion`) and in the authorship runs. **The guarantee is incomplete:** the generic `apply` method is not closed to the assistant (SEC-10).
