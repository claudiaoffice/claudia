`excel-functions`, `win-client`, `import-export-wiring`, `computes-print`, `search`.

- `po/*.po`, `po/claudia.pot`, `web/public/i18n/*.json`, both `claudia.mo`: only reference lines conflicted. Regenerated

  translation of any branch was lost (checked msgid by msgid against all five branches and main).
- `ranges.md`, CHANGELOGs, `human.md`, `updatedAt` lines of task files: both sides kept; the later timestamp wins.
- `human.md`: the branch name is `computes-print` (not "computes-printing").
