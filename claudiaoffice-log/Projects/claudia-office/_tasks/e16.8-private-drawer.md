As a person using a document with others, I want my binder to stay private inside the document, so that I can think freely and share only what I choose.

**Acceptance:** A key domain of its own per person, wrapped only in that person's key slot and outside the shared history; no role, switch or recovery key opens another person's drawer and the key-loss dialog says private notes are lost with the key; others' exports never include a drawer, the author's export only by choice; leaving or removal deletes the drawer, the leave dialog says so and offers an export of the person's own notes, the owner's remove dialog says "any private notes of this person are deleted" without telling whether any exist; sharing an item copies it as an ordinary shared item (history, redactable, author shown, independent) and the original shows "shared on …" to its author only; the AI reads private items only when their owner asks and its answers stay in the drawer; security review and tests for each of these.

**Notes:** Threat model entry in E2.8; ties to E2.2, E2.4 and E2.3.
