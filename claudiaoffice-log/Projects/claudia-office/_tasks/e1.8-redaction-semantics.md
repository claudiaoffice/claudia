As an owner, I want to redact commands, so that a leaked secret can be removed from the history.

**Acceptance:** a redacted command keeps its kind, ids and place in the order but loses its content; the redaction is itself logged without the content; later replay is deterministic; hooks tell caches, undo stacks, AI memory and assets to purge.

**Status evidence:** the contract is decided (D720–D725, superseding D229) and built. `Document.plan_redaction` and
`RedactionPlan` (`packages/claudia-core/src/claudia_core/document.py`) work a redaction out first: the blanked form of each
step (`Command.redacted` / `redacted_in`), the later steps that stop working and are blanked with it, chat messages
(`ChatRecord.blanked`), steps on hidden lines, imports blanked by their app (`DocumentModel.blank`, implemented by the
three apps and `ObjectModel.blank_objects`), whether the document changes now and how it will look. `Document.redact`
carries out a plan and rewrites the store (`DocumentStore.rewrite`). Pictures, object descriptions, import records and
chart titles are blanked too. Tests: `packages/claudia-core/tests/test_redaction_plan.py`,
`packages/claudia-core/tests/test_redaction_content.py`, the apps' `test_*_redacting_an_import.py`. The screens are
E3.13.
