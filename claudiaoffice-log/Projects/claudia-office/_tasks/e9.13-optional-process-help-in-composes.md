As a writer, I want gentle help for the stages of a document (draft, writing, polishing), without being forced into stages.

**Acceptance:** a document may have a stage shown quietly, as metadata only and never a gate; draft turns notes into an outline with the AI's help, writing helps fill it, polishing offers the thinking aids; the AI may suggest a stage change with a reason and a dismissal is remembered so it does not nag; when a suggestion is accepted the person is asked once whether it fits her.

**Notes:** Ground rule: a user never has to follow the stages.
