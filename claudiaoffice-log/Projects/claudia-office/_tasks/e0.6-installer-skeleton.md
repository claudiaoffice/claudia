As a developer, I want a CI job that produces an installable artifact for Linux, Windows and macOS, so that packaging is never a late surprise.

**Acceptance:** CI builds and installs the prototype on clean machines of each OS; signing and notarization needs (accounts, certificates, cost) are listed.

**Notes:** Windows and macOS packages hold the Neutralino shell, the bundled frontend and the server bundle, no GTK. On Linux the natural form may be Flatpak, where the platform supplies GTK; D16's "interpreter always bundled" then needs a decision.

**Status evidence:** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs tests only; no packaging job.
