As a person who chose *suggest*, I want to see what the AI would change, on my own screen and without anyone else being affected, and then accept or reject it, so that I can review before anything is written.

**Acceptance:** a proposal is a stored batch of commands with the revision it was computed at, kept with the chat record; nothing reaches the log before acceptance. Clicking it in the history panel shows the document with the batch applied, for that person only. Accepting replays the batch on the current head as the accepter, tagged `via ai`. The cells or objects it touches are marked (like the AI's presence) but not locked. A proposal turns *outdated* when anything in its footprint changes, and in the first version when any row, column or sheet in its sheet changes; accepting an outdated one shows a conflict prompt, never an automatic rebase, and the AI can redo it against the current state. In suggest mode the AI's read-act-correct loop runs against the scratch copy. No fork, no checkpoint, no merge. In creates the preview shows the proposed objects as ghosts on the slide (E10.6 lists object-level preview as not done, so that is part of this story for creates).

**Notes:** D263. **Spike done 2026-11-11, branch `ai_server`:** `materialize` returns a bare model, which is not enough, but a `Document` around a copy is: `Document.scratch(at=None)` (claudia-core 0.15.0; first numbered 0.13.0 on the branch, renumbered in PROC-09 because styling-suite had taken 0.13.0 and 0.14.0) runs commands, recalculates formulas, reports dependents through its own events, undoes, and shares nothing with the live document in either direction (tests `test_scratch.py`, `test_scratch_workbook.py`). Cost, measured: building the copy is O(size of the state), not of the history (guard in `test_formula_performance.py`): about 80 ms for 6,000 cells and 1.9 s for 60,000 cells with formulas (the formulas are parsed again), against 100 ms and 2.6 s for a replay; one edit on the copy 0.3 ms. So build the copy on demand, once per proposal or per request, and keep it while the preview is open; a lazy or cached copy is an optimisation for very large workbooks, not needed yet. This is a what-if primitive and does not decide D236. Not needed for composes, which keeps its persisted, text-anchored suggestions. Comes **before** the AI writes in computes or creates, because suggest is the default (D262). Needs a screen: `notes/mockups/ai-suggest-mode.svg`.
