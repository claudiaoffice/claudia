As a presenter, I want to see what a template's layouts look like when I choose it, so that I pick a design by looking and not only by name.

**Acceptance:** The cards of *File ▸ New*, the Change template dialog and the list in Server settings ▸ Templates show a row of thumbnails of the template's layouts (the visible ones, the first few and a count), drawn from the layouts themselves, with a text alternative naming each layout. A template of one empty layout shows one empty page. The thumbnails are the same drawing as those in All layouts… (E10.26) and use no stored picture.

**Notes:** Not built: the cards show name, purpose, number of layouts and language. Mocks K, L and M in `claudiaoffice-log/notes/mockups/creates-templates-new-and-server.svg`. Vocabulary: Theme, Template, Layout, Placeholder in `glossary.md`; design in `claudiaoffice-log/notes/creates-templates-architecture.md`.
