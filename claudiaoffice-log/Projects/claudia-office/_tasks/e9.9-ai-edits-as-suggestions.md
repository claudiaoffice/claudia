As an author, I want changes the AI proposes to appear as suggestions I can accept or reject, while colleagues' edits appear immediately.

**Acceptance:** a suggestion is a proposal anchored to a text Region, not a change, shown inline and in the panel; accepting makes a normal command attributed to the accepter with the suggestion's author recorded, rejecting is logged, and a suggestion is marked outdated when its anchored text changes; **the AI has no direct write path into composes text, except for a person who chose *act* (D262, E7.7)**; people's edits are applied immediately; viewers see no suggestions unless the key domains allow it.

**Status evidence:** built — `suggest` (assistant only), `accept_suggestion`, `reject_suggestion`, proposals anchored to a region, acceptance recorded as the accepter's edit with `via=ai`, and `SuggestionList.vue` in the panel. **The "no direct write path" guarantee, fixed 2026-11-06 (SEC-10):** `replace_text` and `set_style` refuse the assistant, and `apply` — inherited from the core table, so it used to bypass both — now refuses `account.system` unconditionally, and is also outside the assistant's allow-list (`apps.MODEL_METHODS["composes"] = ("suggest",)`), checked at dispatch. Also open: undoing a reject reopens the suggestion (intended?), redacting a propose step before its accept may break replay, and the stand-in does not propose around other people's locks.

**2026-11-15 (D640) and 2026-11-16 (E7.10, D680, D681):** the exception of D262 is built. For a person who chose *act* the assistant writes through its allow-list (`apps.MODEL_METHODS`, checked at dispatch): the stand-in through `replace_text`, a language model through `replace_words` and `add_paragraphs`, both through `set_style`; every other write refuses it. For *suggest* it proposes through `suggest` (stand-in) or `suggest_words` (model).
