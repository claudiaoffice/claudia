**Acceptance:** View ▸ Corkboard shows one card per slide with its thumbnail, status (Draft, Building, Polishing; editable), one free-text note, and the planned time per slide with the total in view (D343); dragging a card is the logged move of the slide; empty slides can be planned as empty cards.

**Notes:** Consistent with the slide strip; E10.2 (presentation mode) uses the same planned times.
