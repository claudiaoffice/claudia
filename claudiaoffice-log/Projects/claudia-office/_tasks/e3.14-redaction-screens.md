As an owner (anything) or an editor (my own work), I want to pick steps of the history and chat messages, see what blanking them does, and confirm it, so that a leaked secret is gone without me having to understand the command log.

**Acceptance:** "Redact…" on a history entry and a chat message (owners on anything, editors on their own entries, in all three apps and both kinds of client, with a key shortcut); a dialog that lists what is blanked, names the later steps that go with it, previews the document with the blanked places, says plainly that copies made earlier keep the content, and confirms with a red button that names the scope (Ctrl+Enter); a chat note to the authors concerned, on by default; for an editor whose plan reaches somebody else's work, the reason and "Ask an owner"; for an import, choosing whole objects in the preview; a redacted entry and message look different in the panel; i18n and BITV as everywhere.
