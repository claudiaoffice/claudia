As an editor, I want to turn the mouse wheel over the history and see the document as it was, so that I can find when something changed and who changed it.

**Acceptance:** the wheel over the History & Chat panel steps a playhead; the view shows the state at the step and follows changes, centring one that is out of sight; the filter decides which steps are visited; Ctrl+wheel scrolls; Esc returns to now; chat stays live; nothing in a replay changes the document; keys and the View ▸ Replay history… entry as in D551; in all three apps and every shell.

**Status evidence:** mocked (`notes/mockups/dock-*.svg`); the kernel can already show any revision (`materialize`, `peek_line`). `Document.peek_revision` and the `peek_revision` method give the state at a step and the places it touched (`claudia_core/document.py`, `claudia_server/protocol.py`; tests `test_peek_revision.py`, `test_server_peek_revision.py`). `as_of` answers the views' reads for a step; `BaseSession` has the playhead (`state.replay`, tests `replay-session.test.ts`); the panel has the Replay button, the wheel, Alt+PgUp/PgDn, Alt+Home, Esc, filter, Follow and the newer-changes count; slides and sheets follow what a step touched, the text view scrolls to its paragraph; views are read-only while replaying; View ▸ Replay History… (Ctrl+Alt+Z) is in the manifest, so in every shell. **Not done:** tinting of what changed, "Show differences from now", "Suggest restore…" from a step (restores still go through checkpoints), centring an out-of-sight change in the sheet grid (it scrolls the least needed), a browser test of the wheel.

**Added with D553:** the views mark what the step changed (cells, objects, paragraphs); "Show differences from now" marks what changed after it (`changes_since`); a grid centres an out-of-sight change; "↺ restore this step" suggests a restore to the step on show (`propose_restore_step`, E3.7's agreement). Tests: `test_changes_since.py`, `test_restore_revision.py`, `test_server_as_of.py`, `test_server_restore_live.py`, `replay-session.test.ts`, `replay-tint.test.ts`, `geometry.test.ts`.
