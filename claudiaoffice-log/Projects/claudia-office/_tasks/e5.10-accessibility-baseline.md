**Notes:** D69 names public administration and schools, who in the EU usually have to meet WCAG 2.1 AA through EN 301 549 (BITV 2.0 in Germany). Open bugs: UX-01 (menus), UX-02 (grid), UX-03 (dialogs), UX-04 (contrast), UX-05 (dark mode).
