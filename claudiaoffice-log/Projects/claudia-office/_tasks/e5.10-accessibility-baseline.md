As a user with a screen reader or keyboard only, I want to operate Claudia, so that it is usable by everyone.

**Acceptance:** keyboard reachability of all menus and the history panel; labels for assistive technology; contrast checks; a checklist per release.

**Notes:** D69 names public administration and schools, who in the EU usually have to meet WCAG 2.1 AA through EN 301 549 (BITV 2.0 in Germany). Open bugs: UX-01 (menus), UX-02 (grid), UX-03 (dialogs), UX-04 (contrast), UX-05 (dark mode).

**Status evidence:** the quick fixes of 2026-11-13 are on branch `bitv`, awaiting commit: the page language, document titles, F6 between the parts of the window and the contrast guard (UX-04, UX-28–UX-30; `web/app/lib/regions.ts`, `web/tests/tokens.test.ts`, `web/e2e/regions.cjs`). The grid structure for screen readers (UX-02) is not done; a first estimate of where the product stands by BITV principle, and the order of the rest, is in `notes/bitv-scorecard-2026-11-13.md`.
