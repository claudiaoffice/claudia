As a person who shares a computer with others, I want my private place to answer only my own apps, so that the other
people of this computer cannot see even the titles of my documents.

**Context (2026-11-25, split off from E8.5 by the owner):** with E8.5 a person's apps no longer *mistake* another
person's server for their own (CODE-99), but another account of the same computer can still reach her private server on
purpose (any program may open a port on 127.0.0.1) and see its list of documents: titles and the other unsealed details;
the contents stay sealed. Closing that means starting her server with an access secret and having her shell hand it to
the page, which the web app does not support yet (listing and creating documents send no secret; `open.ts` takes a 401 as
"no documents").

**Acceptance:** another account of the same computer that connects to her private server gets no list and cannot make a
document there; her own apps notice nothing.

**Depends on:** [[e8.5-macos-installer|E8.5 macOS installer]] (the private server and its key).
