As a presenter, I want a small, deliberate set of shapes, so that I make clear slides and not drawings.

**Acceptance:** a shape set chosen with the owner (for example rectangle, rounded rectangle, ellipse, line, arrow, bracket, callout), everything else an image or a diagram; shapes take their colours from the template, and a different colour needs a new named style; pptx/odp import maps unknown shapes to the nearest shape or an image and reports it.

**Status evidence:** three shapes exist as JSON in the built-in library — rectangle, circle, triangle. **The deliberate set is still the owner's decision** (rounded rectangle, line, arrow, bracket, callout?). Colours now come from the document's **palette**: `plain-shape` says `fill: @accent/14` and `stroke: @accent`, and changing the role recolours every shape at once (D200, D201). A template bringing a palette with it is still E10.4.
