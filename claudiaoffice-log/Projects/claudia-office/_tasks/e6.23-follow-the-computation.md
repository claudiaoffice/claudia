As a learner or reviewer, I want to click a cell and see every cell connected to it light up as input, calculation or output, so that I understand where a number comes from and where it goes.

**Acceptance:** clicking a cell that is part of a computation lights the connected cells inside the viewport by role and dims all other cells; no arrows (D297); the roles are shown by shape and a left bar as well as colour; a pill names the clicked cell, the counts per role, the reach (Group or Chain) and closes with Esc; a marker on the sheet tabs says the connection continues on another sheet; dimmed text keeps a contrast of 4.5 : 1; a key shortcut switches following on and off; the same in the web and the GTK client.

**Notes:** Replaces the "highlight precedents and dependents" part of E6.11, which stays open for showing intermediate values. The server half answers `trace`; the screens are `notes/mockups/computes-follow-computation-sheet.svg` and `computes-follow-computation-roles.svg`.

**Status evidence:** the model finds the cells and their roles in `packages/claudia-computes/src/claudia_computes/trace.py` (tests `packages/claudia-computes/tests/test_trace.py`) and the server answers `trace` (`packages/claudia-server/src/claudia_server/computes.py`). Open: the drawing in the web and GTK clients, the tab marker, the shortcut, i18n.
