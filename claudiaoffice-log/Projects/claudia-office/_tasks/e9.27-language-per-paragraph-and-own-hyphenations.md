As a writer of a text in two languages, I want a quotation in English hyphenated as English, and a name of my own broken where I say.

**Acceptance:** a paragraph style can say which language its paragraphs are in (overriding the text's); a person can list words with their hyphenation for a text, kept in the document; both reach the layout and the PDF.

**Notes:** CSS has no property for a language (HTML has the `lang` attribute), so the style needs one of its own or a facet entry; the exceptions belong in the document like the page setup (D904), not in the patterns.
