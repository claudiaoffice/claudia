As a researcher, I want my document to hold its bibliography as BibTeX entries.

**Acceptance:** entries are commands in the log (add, change, remove), undoable and visible in the history; import and export of a `.bib` round-trips field for field, including fields and entry types Claudia does not know, keeping comments and `@string`; citation keys unique, a clash reported and resolved by the person; the entry keeps the original BibTeX text and the display shows decoded text; a bibliography view with search, sort and "cited / not cited"; entries are content-domain data, not shared across documents.

**Notes:** Proposal: BibTeX types and fields as the base, biblatex extras accepted and kept, nothing dropped. Redaction must be able to remove an entry's text.
