As a presenter, I want to place JPEG, PNG, GIF, SVG and other figures.

**Acceptance:** JPEG, PNG, GIF (animated in presentation mode, still in page view), SVG, WebP and AVIF as far as the shell engines support, with a table per engine; other formats converted on the server or refused with a reason; **SVG from outside is always shown as an image, never inline**, and scripts and external references are stripped on export; images are content-addressed assets with alt text required by a prompt; the same image model is reused by composes and computes.

**Notes:** Huge images need size limits and downscaling on the server.
