As a person using any of the three apps, I want the menus to show me only what fits the moment, so that they stay short and I find what I can do.

**Acceptance:** every menu entry and context-menu entry has a rule "fits when" of named conditions (role, server, client, document, selection, ai; D1601), the same in every client; what does not fit is hidden or greyed, one switch per reason and an override per entry in a developer setting, every reason at hide to start (D1606); menu titles never change, an emptied menu or context menu shows one greyed line saying why (D1603); a shortcut of an entry that does not fit says why, and Preferences ▸ Shortcuts lists every action with when it fits (D1604); the shell contract's action state carries whether an entry is shown, and the Linux, Windows and macOS menus hide what the page says; the workbook's "Fits when" column checked by a test (D1605); scenarios first, a screen-reader pass in the trial.

**Notes:** Design in `notes/menus-that-fit.md`. The trial decides whether hiding or greying feels better, per reason, and whether people get the choice.
