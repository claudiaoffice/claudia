As a user, I want named number and date formats, so that values look right for their purpose and my locale.

**Acceptance:** a format is a named definition of its own, not CSS; formats follow the person's locale for display while the stored value is independent of it; mapping to ODS and xlsx formats with losses listed; tests for rounding, negative, large and small numbers, and German and English date formats.

**Notes:** Related to the numeric-semantics decision (E6.4).

**Status (2026-11-13), the `real-world-docs` branch:** the model is built (D348): a workbook's named number formats in
Excel's code, a style's `number_format`, inherited like CSS; the renderer in Python and its TypeScript twin, checked
against shared cases (sections and conditions, colours, rounding half away from zero, scaling, fractions,
scientific, elapsed time, German and English separators and month names, the reader's short date); `TEXT()`;
the grid shows numbers through the words style's format. xlsx and ods formats come in as named formats with
provisional names and go out as number formats and number styles (D349). **Still open:** choosing and editing a format
in the style dialog and a list of formats to name (SVG first), and the locale of `TEXT()` (D342).
