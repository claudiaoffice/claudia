As a user, I want named number and date formats (currency, percent, date, scientific), so that values look right for their purpose and my locale.

Acceptance:

- A format is a named definition of its own, not CSS (D75): kind, pattern or options, and the locale rules; a cell style may refer to one.
- Formats follow the person's locale for display (E5.7) while the stored value is independent of it.
- Mapping to ODS and xlsx number formats; losses are listed (E6.9).
- Tests for rounding, negative numbers, large and small numbers, and date formats in German and English.
