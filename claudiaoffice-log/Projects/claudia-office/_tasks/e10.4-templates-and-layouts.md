As a presenter, I want every slide to follow a layout from a catalogue, so that content and form are separate and a deck stays consistent without a slide master.

**Acceptance:** a *theme* (styles and the palette they refer to), a *layout* (a named arrangement of placeholders and other objects that a slide follows) and a *template* (layouts and a theme, made for a purpose) exist as definitions of their own, not as Things. A placeholder is an object on a layout with a purpose label, unique within the layout; an object on a slide takes it and keeps its frame in step with it. A new presentation starts from a template or from the built-in basic one (one layout with no placeholders, one slide on it); it gets its own copy of the template's layouts and styles and keeps no link to the template, only a note of where it came from. Every slide follows a layout of its own deck. Built-in layouts can only be hidden, unnamed unused layouts disappear, and a deck without slides is a template. Layouts are listed, renamed, ordered, hidden and deleted in *All layouts…*; deleting a layout in use moves its slides to a chosen one, and objects that were in its placeholders keep their place as free boxes.
