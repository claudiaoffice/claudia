As someone who opens a workbook made in Excel or LibreOffice, I want a chart sheet in it to come in as a slide with
its chart, so that the chart I made there is not lost.

**Acceptance:** importing an xlsx file with a chart sheet (or an ods file with a sheet that holds only a chart) gives a
slide at the chart sheet's place in the tab order, named as the chart sheet, with the chart filling its frame; the
loss that today says the chart sheet was left out no longer appears for it. Exporting the workbook again writes the
slide as a sheet with the chart on it (D1473, A9), so a chart sheet does not come back as a chart sheet.

**Notes:** Split off E6.31 step 3 (owner, 2026-11-23): until then an imported chart sheet is skipped with a loss, as
today. A chart sheet holds no cells, which is why the importer leaves it out (`xlsx_read.py`).
