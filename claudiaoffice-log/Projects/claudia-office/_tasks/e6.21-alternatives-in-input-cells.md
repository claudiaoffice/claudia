As a modeller, I want to type {800, 1000, 1300} into an input cell and see every alternative run through the calculations that depend on it, so that I see the range of outcomes of a decision, not one number.

**Acceptance:** an input cell takes `{a, b, c}` (values or formulas) and stores `=ALTERNATIVES(...)` or `=SCENARIOS("name", ...)` (D294); a result cell that depends on alternatives shows its median and spread, a dot strip in the grid and a pop-over with the table of outcomes, the threshold, the sensitivity of each input and the paired mode (D295, D296); sampling above 10 000 combinations says so; the cells keep working under structure edits, undo and several users; Excel and ods files keep the first alternative with a note and read the alternatives back (D298) and the export names what it could not hold (D299); every menu entry has a key shortcut, labels are in the .po files, the controls are usable by keyboard and not by colour alone (BITV); the desktop clients show the same.

**Notes:** Server and model are done on branch `random-in-computes`; the web and the GTK clients are designed (`notes/mockups/computes-alternatives-sheet-and-outcomes.svg`, `computes-alternatives-entry-and-anatomy.svg`) and approved by the owner, but not built. Limits are in `implementation-notes/random-alternatives-follow-computes.md`. No `Simulate()` for now (the owner's call); it would build on the outcome engine.

**Status evidence:** the model builds the alternatives and their outcomes: `packages/claudia-computes/src/claudia_computes/alternatives.py`, `packages/claudia-computes/src/claudia_computes/outcomes.py`, `packages/claudia-computes/src/claudia_computes/formula/forms/alternatives.py`, tests `packages/claudia-computes/tests/test_alternatives_syntax.py` and `packages/claudia-computes/tests/test_alternatives_outcomes.py`; the files carry them through `packages/claudia-computes-io/src/claudia_computes_io/extensions.py`; the server answers `outcome_table` and carries `alts` and `out` in `cells` (`packages/claudia-server/src/claudia_server/computes.py`). The web client draws them: chips and the median with its dot strip in `web/app/components/SheetGrid.vue`, the pop-over in `web/app/components/OutcomePopover.vue`, the logic in `web/app/lib/outcomes.ts` (tests `web/tests/outcomes.test.ts`, `web/tests/session.test.ts`), Tools ▸ Outcomes of this cell… and the cell menu in `web/app/components/Workspace.vue`; the Linux, macOS and Windows menus carry the entry with its key (`clients/linux/src/claudia_shell_gtk/manifest.py`, tests `clients/linux/tests/test_manifest.py`) and the German labels are in `po/de.po`.
