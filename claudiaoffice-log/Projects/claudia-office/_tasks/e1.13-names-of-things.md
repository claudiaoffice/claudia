As a writer, I want every object, slide, sheet and chart tab to have a name of its own, so that I and my colleagues can point at one thing by it.

**Acceptance:** one namespace per document, compared cleaned and case-folded; a name is 1 to 60 characters; new things get a default name in the language of their maker; `RenameThing` with an exact inverse and a refusal that says who has the name; documents from before names still replay, and `name_unnamed` names what has no name; every client can rename (a field in the object's properties, F2 on a tab or a slide).

**Status evidence:** `claudia_core/names.py`, `ObjectModel.named_things`, `RenameThing` and `ThingRenamed` (`claudia_core/objects.py`); slide names in `claudia_creates/model.py`; sheet and chart-tab names in `claudia_computes/model.py` and the commands, with `legacy_names`; the server's `thing_names.py` (`rename_thing`, `name_unnamed`, default names). Tests: `test_core_thing_names.py`, `test_creates_thing_names.py`, `test_computes_thing_names.py`, `test_server_thing_names.py`. **Not done:** the rename fields in the three clients, names in imports and exports (pptx, odp, docx, xlsx: take them from the file, suffix duplicates), names in the AI's view of the document.
