As a writer, I want to refer to a heading, a numbered item or a named thing by its number, its text, its name or its page, so that "see section 2.1" never goes stale when the structure changes.

**Acceptance:** Insert ▸ Cross-Reference… (Ctrl+F2) opens a picker over the whole document (headings as an outline, numbered items, named paragraphs and objects) with "Show as" (number, text, number and text, page, name); the reference is one inline field at a U+FFFC that stores the target's thing id and what to show; what it shows is derived in the kernel and changes for everybody when the target is renumbered or retyped; the page is filled in by the layout in page view and print; a reference whose target is gone is shown as broken, undo mends it, and going to it replays the history to the target's last moment; Ctrl+click or Ctrl+Alt+Enter goes to the target; the field kinds are a registry in `claudia_core` that citations (E9.16) and page fields (E9.14) will use; ODT bookmark-ref and docx REF/PAGEREF fields both ways, losses reported.

**Notes:** Design in `notes/e9-composes-outline-references-toc.md` (sections 5, 6; slices S5, S6), screens `notes/mockups/composes-references.svg`. Depends on E9.22 for numbers; the field mechanism itself does not. A contenteditable spike for the atom comes first (Firefox and Chromium).
