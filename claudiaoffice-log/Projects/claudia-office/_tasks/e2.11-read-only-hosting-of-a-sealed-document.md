As a viewer, I want to open a document that nobody has opened since the server last let go of its keys, so that I do not have to wait for an editor.

**Acceptance:** a viewer who is first to log in reads the document from its snapshot, read-only, with the content key alone; when an editor or owner arrives and brings the live document up, the viewer's window is handed over to it without a reload; a viewer never receives anything from the history or chat on the way (D1261); a document without a usable snapshot still answers `sealed`.

**Notes:** Split out of E2.4 by D1262. Moves up if a deployment shows that viewers are often first.
