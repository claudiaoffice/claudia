As a presenter, I want to see all the layouts of my deck and to rename, order, hide and delete them, so that the catalogue my slides follow stays tidy without a slide master.

**Acceptance:** *Format ▸ Layout ▸ All layouts…* lists every layout of the deck with a thumbnail, what it is for, how many slides follow it, and a badge for hidden ones, "unused" and "to name" (a provisional layout nobody has named); "Only the ones waiting for a name" filters the list. The chosen layout shows its placeholders (purpose and style) and the other objects on it. Rename, Move up, Move down and Hide work on any layout; Delete works on a layout of the document only, and asks where its slides go: objects that took a placeholder with the same purpose follow it, the others become free boxes at their present frame, and a built-in layout offers Hide instead (D1535). Deleting is one step, Undo brings the layout and the slides back. The first visible layout is the first in list order. The list has a list alternative for reading with a screen reader (BITV). Same in all three apps.

**Notes:** Not built in E10.4 (the model, `settle` and the commands exist; there is no screen). Mocks I and J in `claudiaoffice-log/notes/mockups/creates-layouts-edit-and-manage.svg`. Parallels All styles… (D302). Keys were proposed as Ctrl+Shift+Alt+E and are not in `claudia-menus.xlsx`; AltGr on German keyboards forbids them, so they need new ones. The delete dialog's "Show the preview…" is E10.28. Vocabulary: Theme, Template, Layout, Placeholder in `glossary.md`; design in `claudiaoffice-log/notes/creates-templates-architecture.md`.
