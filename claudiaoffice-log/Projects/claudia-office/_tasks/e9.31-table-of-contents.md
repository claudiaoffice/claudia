As a writer, I want a table of contents that is always right, so that readers find their way and I never have to update it.

**Acceptance:** Insert ▸ Table of Contents (Ctrl+Shift+Alt+I) puts a "Contents" paragraph (style Contents Title, no outline level) and a generated paragraph at the caret in one step; the generated paragraph stores only its options (levels, numbers, page numbers, leader) and cannot be typed into; its entries are derived from the outline levels of E9.22 and change for everybody when a heading changes; Flow view shows the entries as links without page numbers; page view, print and PDF show page numbers found by the layout in at most three passes, with a dot leader; the look is the styles Contents 1–9; exports write the entries without page numbers, docx with `updateFields`, ODT reporting `toc-pages`.

**Notes:** Design in `notes/e9-composes-outline-references-toc.md` (section 7; slices S3, S4), screens `notes/mockups/composes-table-of-contents.svg`. Depends on E9.22 (outline levels). Later generated kinds: the reference list of E9.16 and lists of figures.
