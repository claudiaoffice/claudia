As an owner, I want a catalog of everything a person can do in each app, checked against what the ai can do and see, so that no feature is added without deciding what the ai does with it.

**Acceptance:**
- *One action catalog per app.* One entry per protocol method. The menu workbook (`claudia-menus`) gets per-app "Methods" columns, and a test fails for a menu entry that names no method, a method that does not exist, or a method no entry and no catalog line knows.
- *Two questions per action: can the ai do it, and can it see what it works on.* Doing: the request is offered to the model and taught in a skill. Seeing: every property the request sets or depends on (rotation, order, fill, how a shape looks unturned) shows in what the lens tells the model, or is listed with a reason why not.
- *Reserved for people, with a reason on record:* permissions and ownership, undo, restore and setting a checkpoint, redaction, installing or running code, and view-only state (zoom, layout, palette, language). Reading the history and the chat is not reserved (it is a gap to close, see below).
- *A file per app next to `skills.md` (`ai/actions.md`)* with one line per action and its state for doing and for seeing; a generated overview across the three apps in `claudiaoffice-log/notes/`. The test regenerates both lists and compares them in both directions. The number of gaps may fall but never rise, and every new method or property is classified in the commit that adds it.
- *No model in the test.* The checks are consistency checks and never call a model; the model is tried by hand before delivery ("use it before delivery"), and the result reported, not gated.
- *First slices that close gaps:* numbered and bulleted lists in composes (the ai cannot make a numbered list), and rotating in creates with the rotation and the look of each shape shown to the model (the ai could not tell which way a triangle points).
- *Not in this story:* a picture of a slide for the model; new shapes (E10.7); the ai reading the document's history and chat (own story).
