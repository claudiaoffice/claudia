As a writer, I want long words broken with a hyphen at the end of a line in my pages, so that justified text does not gape and ragged text stays even.

**Acceptance:** words are hyphenated by the patterns of the text's language where a style says `hyphens: auto`, and at soft hyphens typed into them; the lines of a paragraph are chosen together; the rules are the same on every machine; a text without a language, or one the server has no patterns for, is not hyphenated and the preview says so.

**Status evidence (2026-11-12):** built. `layout/src/text/hyphenate.ts` (Liang, TeX's hyph-utf8 patterns) and the Knuth–Plass breaker in `layout/src/text/paragraph.ts` (D912); patterns served by `claudia_server.hyphenation` (D913); the text's language in `claudia_composes` (`SetLanguage`, D915); `hyphens` in `claudia_core.css` (CSS_VERSION 4); Language in the print preview's bar, Hyphenate in the style editor, Ctrl+- for a soft hyphen (D916). German, British and American English.
