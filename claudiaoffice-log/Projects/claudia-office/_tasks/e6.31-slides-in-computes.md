As someone who builds charts in a workbook, I want a slide among my sheets that holds a chart together with text boxes and shapes, so that a chart can have a page of its own with words beside it, and works like a slide in creates.

**Acceptance:** a tab of a computes document shows a sheet or a slide (glossary: Tab, Slide), in one tab order, so a slide can sit between two sheets and be moved like one. A slide is the same holder as in creates: a fixed 16:9 frame with an object layer, objects anchored by position; its type, frame and anchor are shared in the core, as the paragraph is, and each app keeps its own slides (creates: notes, times, presenting; computes: its tab order). A chart is an object on a sheet or a slide, specified by its data, kind and titles, with a definition of its own: copying a chart copies it, deleting the chart deletes it. A slide comes into existence only for a chart: Edit ▸ Chart… offers "Show as: A slide" where it offered "A tab" today (while a slide is shown: "A slide" or "On this slide"), and a chart on a sheet has "Move to Slide" in its object menu, which makes a slide right after the sheet and moves the chart there in one step; the new slide gets the next free generic name "Slide n" and the chart keeps its name (owner, 2026-11-23); on the slide the chart first fills the frame and stays an ordinary object. There is no other way to add a slide; "+" in the tab strip adds sheets only (owner, 2026-11-22). A slide goes with its last object, in the same undo step (Q20); deleting a sheet or a slide by hand is UX-68. The chart tab (computes' `Chart`, `add_chart`, `remove_chart`) is gone in the same step that brings this stand-in. A computes slide is written to xlsx and ods as an empty worksheet with its objects on it, with the loss "slide"; it reads back as a sheet. Last step: the chart names of E1.18 (kind `chart`, `ChartDefinition`, the asset kind `chart`), which can only take the stored key `charts` once the chart tabs are gone.

**Steps** (agreed 2026-11-22), each ending green and usable: (1) the slide's type, frame and anchor move into the core and creates uses them, nothing visible changes; (2) computes has slides: in one tab order with the sheets, shapes, text boxes and images on a slide, export as an empty worksheet with the loss "slide", the chart tabs still beside them (no way yet for a person to make a slide); (3) the chart tab becomes a slide: Edit ▸ Chart… offers "A slide" instead of "A tab", "Move to Slide" for a chart on a sheet, one spec per chart object, and computes' `Chart`, `add_chart`, `remove_chart` and the tab list `charts` go in the same step; (4) the chart names of E1.18 (Q17 of the session), now that `charts` is free.
