As someone who builds charts in a workbook, I want a slide among my sheets that holds a chart together with text boxes and shapes, so that a chart can have a page of its own with words beside it, and works like a slide in creates.

**Acceptance:** a tab of a computes document shows a sheet or a slide (glossary: Tab, Slide), in one tab order, so a slide can sit between two sheets and be moved like one. A slide is the same holder as in creates: a fixed 16:9 frame with an object layer, objects anchored by position; its type, frame and anchor are shared in the core, as the paragraph is, and each app keeps its own slides (creates: notes, times, presenting; computes: its tab order). A chart is an object on a sheet or a slide, specified by its data, kind and titles, with a definition of its own: copying a chart copies it, deleting the chart deletes it. Edit ▸ Chart… offers a new slide where it offered a tab today, and a chart on a sheet can be moved onto a new slide, where it first fills the visible frame and stays an ordinary object. The chart tab (computes' `Chart`, `add_chart`, `remove_chart`) is gone in the same step that brings this stand-in. A computes slide is written to xlsx and ods as an empty worksheet with its objects on it, with the loss "slide"; it reads back as a sheet. Last step: the chart names of E1.18 (kind `chart`, `ChartDefinition`, the asset kind `chart`), which can only take the stored key `charts` once the chart tabs are gone.

**Notes:** Grilled with the owner on 2026-11-22 (questions Q1–Q18 of the session on chart and diagram): "diagram" is not a concept of claudia's own (a Mermaid diagram is a container); German says "Diagramm" for a chart. Mockups, architecture, specs, branch and decision range follow the project's process. Existing documents with chart tabs are not upgraded (pre-release, E1.16).
