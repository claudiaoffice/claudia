As a user who exchanges workbooks with Excel or LibreOffice, I want my cell styles to arrive there under their purpose names, and named cell styles from there to arrive in claudia.

**Acceptance:** each named style that cells use becomes an xlsx named cell style (`cellStyles`) plus the `cellXfs` the cells point at, and the ODS equivalent; on import a named cell style becomes a claudia style of the same name as far as the allow-list takes it, and direct cell formats become provisional styles named by what differs, waiting in the to-name list (D349; first written as "listed as unmapped", D314); a workbook nobody styled still round-trips as it does today; test documents opened by hand in Excel and LibreOffice.

**Notes:** Deliberately not in the styling-suite round (D314). Depends on E6.13.

**Status (2026-11-13), the `real-world-docs` branch:** the acceptance changed with D349 (the owner chose provisional
styles over unmapped direct formats). Import of xlsx and ods: Normal/Default as Body text, named styles by name, direct
formats as provisional styles named by what differs, style ranges for cells, rows and columns, `look` losses per thing
no style holds. A new xlsx or ods file writes Body text as Normal/Default, named styles as named cell styles and the
rest as direct formats on top. Checked locally against the owner's two real workbooks (kept out of the repository)
and in LibreOffice. A cell, row or column restyled in an *edited* xlsx or ods gets a new look of its own; nothing the file had
changes (D350). **Still open:** grouping the to-name list of a large import (E9.7); test documents opened by hand in
Excel.
