As a writer, I want any style to be able to be a heading of a level, and headings numbered from their style, so that chapters number themselves and a table of contents and references can find them.

**Acceptance:** a paragraph style has an outline level (1–9 or none), inherited along `based_on`, and a paragraph is a heading because of it (no list of heading names in the model or the layout); Heading 3–6 are built-in; a style can point at a list, so its paragraphs are items of that list at their outline level; Format ▸ List ▸ Number Headings turns numbering on for every style with an outline level in one step; numbering schemes are document data (built-ins Bullets, Numbers, Chapters) with nine level formats, edited in Format ▸ List ▸ All Numbering…; a label carries the number a reference prints ("2.3"); the editor gives headings `aria-level`; list markers and heading numbers are drawn in the print layout too; ODT and docx keep outline levels and style numbering.
