As a writer, I want numbered headings and references to a heading, a paragraph or another anchor that never go stale, so that I do not have to refresh or retype numbers.

**Acceptance:** a style can name a numbering scheme and a level, so a numbered heading is a style switch and not a per-paragraph setting; headings feed the table of contents; a reference stores an anchor id and which part of its label to show, follows the anchor when it moves, and is shown as broken when the anchor is gone; an "All numbering…" dialog beside "All styles…" manages schemes, the styles and lists that use them.

**Notes:** Depends on E9.21 (the derived label and the scheme registry). Related: E9.12 outline view, E9.16 citations (inline items that point at something), E9.14 page numbering fields. The concept is section 4 of `notes/e9-composes-lists.md`; the enumeration UI needs its own SVG design first.
