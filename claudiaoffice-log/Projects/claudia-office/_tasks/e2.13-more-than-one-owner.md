As an owner of a document that matters to a team, I want to make an editor a co-owner, so that the document does not depend on me alone when I am away.

**Deferred (D1413, undecided).** Ready to be built once the owner decides; until then new work does not assume a document has only one owner.

**Scope:** *Make co-owner* (an owner proposes, the editor accepts with her own password) and *Step down* (an owner becomes an editor with her own password if another owner remains), in the document file, on the server and in the Role dialog of the web and desktop clients; several owners in the list of people; the strings that say "the owner" reworded, with the German catalogs; the menu workbook; E2E scenarios agreed first.

**Open before building:** what a co-owner may do alone, and what needs every owner (deleting the document, a new recovery key, removing people, agreeing to a restore, D1114).
