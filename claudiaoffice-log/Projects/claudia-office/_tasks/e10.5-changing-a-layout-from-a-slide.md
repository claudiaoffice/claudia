As a presenter, I want to be offered to update every slide with the same layout, or to make a new layout, when I change something on a slide that belongs to the layout, so that my slides stay consistent without me opening a master.

**Acceptance:** a change on a slide to anything connected to its layout, a placeholder or another object of the layout, is detected as a layout change; free objects never trigger it. The slide at once follows a new provisional layout named after its parent, and a strip offers *Update layout · n slides instead*, *New layout from this slide* (asking what the layout is for) and *Undo*; there is no "only this slide" and no slide that differs from its layout. Updating more than one slide first shows a preview (thumbnails Before | After with the changed frame outlined, and a list alternative for accessibility). Deleting a placeholder on a slide is offered the same way; on the other slides objects filled in it stay as free boxes and empty ones disappear, and the preview says so. Changing a slide's layout lets objects follow the placeholder with the same purpose, and the others become free boxes where they are. Pasting between decks matches layouts and styles by name.

**Notes:** Design agreed in the grill of 2026-11-22/23: `claudiaoffice-log/notes/creates-templates-architecture.md` and the mocks `claudiaoffice-log/notes/mockups/creates-layouts-*.svg`, `creates-templates-new-and-server.svg`, `creates-architecture-*.svg`. Vocabulary: Theme, Template, Layout, Placeholder in `glossary.md`. Layout objects (a logo, a footer) are not copied onto slides: they are drawn from the layout, can be selected on a slide, and any change to them is offered like a change to a placeholder. For an object that takes a placeholder, moving, resizing, rotating, deleting and its own look trigger the offer; typing and text formatting never do. The strip appears when the user leaves the box. Usability-test with people who know PowerPoint. Supersedes the "template provenance on every slide" idea: no slide shows inherited properties, since no slide differs.
