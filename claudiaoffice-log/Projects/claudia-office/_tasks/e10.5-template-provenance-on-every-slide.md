As a presenter, I want every slide to show which template and layout it comes from and what is inherited.

**Acceptance:** a badge or panel on the selected slide names the template, the layout and any named variant, showing inherited properties as inherited with a link to change them in the template; trying to edit an inherited item explains where it is defined and offers to open the template or create a named variant; a list of slides that deviate from their layout.

**Notes:** The direct answer to PowerPoint's master-slide confusion; usability-test it with people who know PowerPoint.

**Mock-ups for review (2026-11-13):** the template line and the layout chip, the inherited-item popover with
*Change it in the template…* and *Make a named variant…*, and the list of slides that differ from their layout
(`claudiaoffice-log/notes/mockups/creates-templates-slide.svg` panels A–B, `creates-templates-import-and-new.svg`
panel H). Only place and size may differ (D373).
