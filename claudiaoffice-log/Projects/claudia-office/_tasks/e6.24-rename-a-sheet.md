As a table maker, I want to rename a sheet, so that its name says what it holds.

**Acceptance:** the sheet's name changes through `RenameThing`; every formula that reads the sheet by name is rewritten with it and put back by undo, in one step; named ranges, tables and chart ranges that point at the sheet follow; the name must be free in the whole document (D550); a rename that would break a formula is refused with a sentence.

**Status evidence:** `WorkbookModel.rename_sheet` prints again every formula that reads the sheet (`formula.rewrite.reprinted`), and `RenameThing` undoes it exactly (a `Batch` of the old name and the typed sources). Tests: `test_computes_thing_names.py`, `test_server_thing_names.py`, `session.test.ts`. Rename UI: double-click a sheet tab or Edit ▸ Rename Sheet… (Ctrl+Shift+F11), the same for slides in creates (Rename Slide…, E6.24 asked for "sheets in creates and computes": in creates a slide is the sheet); chart tabs can be renamed the same way. A formula that names a sheet that does not exist is stored as `#REF!` and does not wait for the name.

**Notes:** named ranges, tables and chart ranges hold the sheet by id, so they follow without a rewrite.
