As a table maker, I want to rename a sheet, so that its name says what it holds.

**Acceptance:** the sheet's name changes through `RenameThing`; every formula that reads the sheet by name is rewritten with it and put back by undo, in one step; named ranges, tables and chart ranges that point at the sheet follow; the name must be free in the whole document (D550); a rename that would break a formula is refused with a sentence.

**Notes:** formulas hold the sheet's name as text today, so a rename needs the rewrite that `rename_name` does for named ranges. Until then `RenameThing` on a sheet is refused with "Sheets cannot be renamed yet".
