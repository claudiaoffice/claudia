As an administrator, I want to add and remove the templates of my server, and as a presenter I want to save my own presentation as a template, so that the people on a server start from its designs.

**Acceptance:** a template is a Claudia export of a presentation without slides; there is no template file type. *Save as template…* (anyone) writes one, with content only and no history or chat, as a logged export. Server settings ▸ Templates (administrator) adds one after a check (an export of a creates presentation, no slides, at least one layout, a unique name) and removes one; the built-in basic template cannot be removed. *File ▸ New* shows the templates as cards by purpose, the basic one first. Starting a deck imports the template as a new sealed document owned by the starter, not logged as an exit. Template texts of the server stay in the administrator's language with a stated language; texts of the built-in template are translated.

**Notes:** Design agreed in the grill of 2026-11-22/23: `claudiaoffice-log/notes/creates-templates-architecture.md` and the mocks `claudiaoffice-log/notes/mockups/creates-layouts-*.svg`, `creates-templates-new-and-server.svg`, `creates-architecture-*.svg`. Vocabulary: Theme, Template, Layout, Placeholder in `glossary.md`. Same shape as Server settings ▸ Fonts (D1041). A `.potx` becomes a template by importing it as a deck and saving it as a template (E10.24). Official, locked templates are a separate story (E12.7).
