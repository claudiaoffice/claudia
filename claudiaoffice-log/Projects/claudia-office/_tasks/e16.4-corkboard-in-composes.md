As a writer, I want my chapters as cards on a board with a status and a note, so that I see the whole text and how far each part is, and can rearrange it.

**Acceptance:** View ▸ Corkboard shows one card per chapter or section; the title is read from the document and read-only on the card; each card has a status chip (icon and word; defaults Draft, Writing, Polishing, editable per document) and one free-text note that shows grey priming text when empty; dragging a card is the logged `move_section` command with renumbering and the text follows; empty cards are allowed; status and note are stored as logged commands in the content key domain; an open-point badge shows when E16.9 is present; keyboard-operable (BITV); export to ODT and DOCX loses nothing of the text and the way status and notes are exported or dropped is stated in E16.10.

**Notes:** Related: E9.12 (outline view) shares the structure; the corkboard adds status and note.
