As a developer, I want to compare LaTeX renderers on real samples, so that we pick the engine for help pages, tooltips and formula pop-ups.

**Acceptance:** about 20 sample formulas and 5 sample help pages rendered by KaTeX or MathJax, MicroTeX and Tectonic, recording render time, SVG quality, bundle size, offline operation, behaviour on all three OSes and shell-escape settings; **for D88** a full-document test with a sample article (sections, lists, tables, figures, `amsmath`, citations through BibTeX and through biblatex/biber) and a Beamer deck compiled offline by each candidate that can, recording the offline package set and how it is curated, compile time warm and cold, memory, bundle size, the PDF-to-SVG route and the sandbox; **for the preview question** the latency of a single formula in the full engine against KaTeX or MicroTeX.

**Status evidence:** one probe on one Linux machine with Debian TeX Live 2023: formula to PDF 0.15 s (pdflatex) and 0.32 s (xelatex), PDF to SVG 0.03 s, peak memory about 183 MB — so a full engine is probably fast enough for formulas. Sandbox flags stop `\write18`, absolute and `..` reads and writes outside the job. XeTeX ignores fontconfig aliases (D95). **Not done:** Tectonic, KaTeX/MicroTeX, every full-document test; that machine had no biblatex, biber, dvisvgm or tectonic, so biblatex and Beamer-with-biber remain untested.
