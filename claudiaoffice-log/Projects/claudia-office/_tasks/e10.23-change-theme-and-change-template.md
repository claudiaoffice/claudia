As a presenter, I want to give a finished deck another look, or another set of layouts, from a template, so that a rebrand does not mean rebuilding slides.

**Acceptance:** *Change theme…* replaces styles and palette by purpose name and leaves the layouts alone; *Change template…* also matches layouts by name. Unmatched styles and layouts of the deck stay. Objects of a slide follow the placeholder with the same purpose in the new layout and otherwise become free boxes where they are. A preview shows the result before anything changes. The deck keeps no link to the template it was changed to.

**Notes:** Design agreed in the grill of 2026-11-22/23: `claudiaoffice-log/notes/creates-templates-architecture.md` and the mocks `claudiaoffice-log/notes/mockups/creates-layouts-*.svg`, `creates-templates-new-and-server.svg`, `creates-architecture-*.svg`. Vocabulary: Theme, Template, Layout, Placeholder in `glossary.md`. Themes are listed from the templates on the server (a library of templates only). It is one command made of final values, so undo is one step.
