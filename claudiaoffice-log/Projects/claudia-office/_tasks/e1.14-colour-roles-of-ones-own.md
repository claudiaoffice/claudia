As a writer, I want to add a colour role of my own to a document's palette (say "Warning" or "Our brand blue"), so that a style or a shape can name what the colour means and one change recolours everything that signifies it.

**Acceptance:** a person can add a role to the document's palette, giving it a name, a purpose in words and a colour; the role is part of the document (the palette is, D200) and travels with it between servers; a style or a shape can name it like any of the nine built-in roles (`@warning`, `@warning/55`), and the picker lists it by name and purpose with the contrast warning of D202; a role can be renamed and its colour changed, and the change recolours everything that names it, as one undoable step per author; a role cannot be deleted while a style or an object names it unless the person chooses where those go (the rule of D226 for styles: assign another role, or keep the current look as a literal colour), and the dialog says beforehand what a change will cost, in words and never as a ratio (D210); the nine built-in roles stay and can be recoloured, not deleted; the same in the Colours dialog of all three apps; an Office export writes the resolved colours, and an import never creates a role by itself.

**Notes:** Cut out of E1.10 on 2026-11-19 (D1302): the style model is closed without it. Today `core.SetRole` recolours one of the nine roles and `core.ReplacePalette` brings a different set; nothing adds a tenth. **Open, to ask when this is taken up:** how many roles at most (the picker lists them all); whether a role is named and cleaned like a thing (D550) or in a namespace of its own; whether a template can carry roles (D200 says the palette may later come with a template); what `ReplacePalette` does with a role of the person's own (keep it, or refuse while it is in use); and whether an imported document that uses a colour many times offers to make it a role (the idea of E9.8's clean-up for styles). It belongs to the colour axis (D200 to D202), not to the style model.

**Status evidence:** none: nothing is built. Before it is started it needs the owner's mock for the Colours dialog (add, rename, delete) in all three apps, as every new screen does.
