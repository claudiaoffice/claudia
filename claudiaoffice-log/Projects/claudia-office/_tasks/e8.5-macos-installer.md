As a person with a Mac, I want to install claudia from one package, so that the three apps and their server run without
my installing Python, Node or anything else.

**Agreed with the owner (2026-11-25, grilling round 1 and 2):**

* An **unsigned `.pkg`** for **Apple Silicon** only, **macOS 14** and newer; everything that needs no Apple Developer
  membership is in scope (no signing, no notarization). The README and the download page say, in English and German, how to
  open an unsigned package (System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ "Open Anyway").
* Installs **for all users** (administrator password): three apps **claudia composes**, **claudia computes** and **claudia
  creates** directly in `/Applications`; the shared parts (server, Node, client) in one system folder people do not open.
  No AI runtime (as D745). Installer pages in English and German.
* **The server as a service**: an option of the installer, off by default; started by the Mac for everyone. A **place** of
  its own, never taken for a person's own server (see below).
* **Updates** install over an older version; a running service is restarted, its settings stay.
* **Uninstall** with an "uninstall claudia" app in `/Applications/Utilities`: removes the apps, the shared parts and the
  service; documents and each person's own settings stay, and it says where.
* **A person's apps only ever use her own server** ("This computer", glossary "Place"), on all three systems: fixes
  CODE-99 and CODE-100. Changes D445, D504 and D743.
* **Built on the owner's Mac** by `packaging/macos/build.sh` (`--release vX.Y.Z` attaches it to the release); a
  `macos.yml` workflow is written and switched off until a runner service with Macs is connected. **Tested** by a script
  that installs, checks and uninstalls, run in a macOS virtual machine (UTM or Tart).

**Acceptance:** the package installs, every app opens a window with its own server, the service (when chosen) serves
documents to every person of the Mac as a place of its own, an update keeps documents and settings, and the uninstaller
leaves only documents and each person's settings; the test script passes in a VM.

**Open (grilling):** the service as a place (how people find it, its administrator, who may use it), and how a person's
apps tell their own server from another one.
