As a presenter, I want to lay out my thoughts as a connected map and turn a thought, or a chain, into slides.

**Acceptance:** a thought map view in the frontend; a node or a path becomes a slide or a sequence in a chosen layout, and the slide keeps a link to its node; the map and the slide order stay in sync through commands, not two stores; presentation mode can later follow a path instead of the slide order.

**Notes:** Inspired by Prezi; the zooming canvas itself is not a goal. A thought map could share a model with the composes outline (E9.12).
