As a creates user, I want to find and replace text in a presentation with the same panel, so that I rename a product or a date on every slide at once.

**Acceptance:** the `SearchHost` of creates searches the words of text boxes, shape labels, speaker notes and the descriptions of objects, on all slides or on this slide; each result shows the slide, the object and the words around the match, and a click goes to it; an object selected by another person is skipped and named (D47, D452); a replacement keeps the object's box and paragraph styles; Replace all is one command and one undo step.

**Notes:** Depends on E1.12 and E5.15. Notes and descriptions are searched too (owner, 2026-11-14: all objects and texts, including speaker notes; D450). Shown in `notes/mockups/search-in-composes-and-creates.svg`.
