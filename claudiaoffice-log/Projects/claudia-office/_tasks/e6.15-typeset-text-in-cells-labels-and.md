As a scientist or teacher, I want a cell, a label or a chart title to show typeset LaTeX.

**Acceptance:** a cell, text label or chart title can be marked as LaTeX and is rendered by the typesetting service and drawn as an image, with the source staying the cell content that formulas and search see; the `=$$\frac{x}{y}$$` cases work; rendering never blocks the grid; output is cached and shown as an image, never inline.

**Notes:** Depends on E3.4 and E6.3. A sheet with thousands of typeset cells needs batching and limits.

**Checked against the code, 2026-11-17 (`main` at `6814bac`).** A rendered text container (LaTeX, Mermaid, Typst) can float on a sheet as an object (E12.5, D390), which covers a typeset label beside the cells; typeset text *in* a cell or a chart title is still not built.
