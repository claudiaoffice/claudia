As a person writing a text, I want to ask `@ai` to tighten a paragraph, add a heading or fix the spelling and have it done (or proposed, in suggest mode), so that composes has the same AI as computes and creates and not only a scripted stand-in.

**Acceptance:** with a language model on the server, `@ai` in a text is answered by the model-driven loop, through a document lens like the workbook and slide lenses: it is told how paragraphs and words are named, the paragraph styles, the headings, the start of the text and the paragraph the asker's cursor is in, all fenced as data; it may read paragraphs, the outline, the styles and the open suggestions; in *act* it changes words, adds paragraphs and sets a paragraph's style, and after each change gets the touched paragraphs as they are now; in *suggest* it only proposes, as anchored suggestions people accept or reject; it never counts characters; a misquote fails with a message that lets it read again and correct. Without a model the stand-in answers as before. The panel's switch works the same in all three apps.

**Status evidence:** built 2026-11-16 on branch `composes-ai`: the lens `packages/claudia-server/src/claudia_server/document_lens.py` (`DocumentLens`, `TextRecorder`), the methods `packages/claudia-server/src/claudia_server/composes_words.py` (`paragraphs`, `outline`, `replace_words`, `add_paragraphs`, `suggest_words`), the allow-list and `NOT_FOR_A_MODEL` in `packages/claudia-server/src/claudia_server/apps.py`, `Lens.orient` with `asker` and `writes`, on top of D640 (the AI's writes in composes, the stand-in acting, the panel's note removed). The help topic `composes.asking-ai-to-review` (en, de) has a section on asking a language model.
