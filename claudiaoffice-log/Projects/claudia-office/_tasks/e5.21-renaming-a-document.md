As the owner of a document, I want to give it another name, so that its name says what it is now, without anybody losing it from their lists.

**Acceptance:** the sealed parts of a new document are bound to its uid alone (`claudia-container`: `Manifest.binding` is the uid); `rename_document(password, name)` renames it for its owner only, refuses a name another document of the server has, renames the file and the manifest in one step, and tells the people connected, whose pages reconnect under the new name; Details… on the front page and File ▸ Properties… in the document offer the rename to the owner (and say to everybody else who may rename it); Mine, collections and the shells' lists keep the uid and follow a rename through `/api/docs/lookup`; the Details dialog no longer says the name is sealed into the file; menus in all three shells, a key shortcut, i18n, tests first.

**Notes:** Decided 2026-11-15 (D536) after the owner found the Details dialog saying "The name is sealed into the file and cannot change": the uid (D533) was meant to make the name free. Open for the build: what happens to a rename while another person has the document open (refuse, or rename and reconnect everybody); whether the import's "a document called … is here already" then offers to bring it in under another name (a placed file keeps its uid, so it would be renamed in the manifest only).
