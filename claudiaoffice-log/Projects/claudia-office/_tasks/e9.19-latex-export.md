As a researcher, I want to save my composes document as a LaTeX project, so that I can send it to a journal or a colleague who works in LaTeX. 

Acceptance:
- Save As produces a zip with a main .tex file, the bibliography as .bib (from E9.15, unchanged), the figures as files, and, when needed, the class or style files chosen for the export; nothing needs the network.
- Mapping (documented, with a list of losses): headings and named styles to sectioning commands and environments (the document's styles map to commands in a preamble the person can edit); lists, tables, footnotes, quotes; maths and typeset blocks as written (E12.5); citations to \cite with the chosen citation package; figures with captions; page style (E9.14) to class options and geometry as far as it maps.
- The export compiles with the bundled engine (E3.10) as a test of every release; a document with every construct in the mapping is the fixture.
- Authorship, suggestions and comments are not exported by default; an option may add them as margin notes.
- The same engine can produce the PDF of the document directly (typeset PDF, see E9.4); the zip and the PDF come from the same .tex.
