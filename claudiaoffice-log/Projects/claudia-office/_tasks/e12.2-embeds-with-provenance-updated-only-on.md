As an author, I want to insert a result from another document and keep a link to its source, so that the evidence travels with the story.

**Acceptance:** the person's client opens both documents and pulls a rendered result into the target; the embedded item carries document id, server, range or chart name and the source revision, shown on hover and in a list of sources; **updates only happen after asking**, with the prompt showing what changed and "not now" remembered until the source changes again; no server-to-server access and no shared keys; works between all app pairs and within one app, as an undoable command; if the source cannot be opened the item keeps its last content and says why.

**Notes:** Revision ids can change meaning after a restore, so provenance must record the line too. Needs a cheap way to ask a source for its latest revision.
