As a composes user, I want to find and replace text in a document with the same panel as in computes, so that I correct a term everywhere and see where it sits.

**Acceptance:** the `SearchHost` of composes searches paragraphs of the whole document, a section or the selection, optionally by style; each result shows its heading and the words around the match; whole word is available; a replacement takes on the style of the text it replaces and adds no direct formatting; Replace all is one command and one undo step; a match inside another person's lock zone (D11) is skipped and named; matches move while others type, so a match whose text changed is skipped; the part "a word" is offered in the builder.

**Notes:** Depends on E1.12 and E5.15. Footnotes, citations and fields are not searched until they exist (D287). Shown in `notes/mockups/search-in-composes-and-creates.svg`.
