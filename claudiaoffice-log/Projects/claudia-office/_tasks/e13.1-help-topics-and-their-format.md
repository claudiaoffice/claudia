As a user, I want help written as short "how do I…" topics with a worked example each.

**Acceptance:** a topic has an id, a title phrased as a task, the apps it applies to, a language, tags, a short body of steps, **at least one worked example**, links to related topics and links to what it explains; the authoring format and rendering decided with E0.4; size rules so a small local model can read a topic whole, and an author guide; topics versioned with the thing they describe and flagged stale when the subject changes; no executable content.

**Status evidence:** one topic exists as a **draft shape** — `claudia_typeset/help/configure-latex.md` (id `typeset.configure-latex`), Markdown with simple front matter, with tests checking the id, a title phrased as a question, the metadata, the worked example, that it names both settings and the pitfalls, and a size cap so a small model can read it whole. **The format itself is undecided** and the file is to be converted once E0.4 settles the renderer.

**Checked against the code, 2026-11-17 (`main` at `6814bac`).** The format is decided by the code: `claudia_server.help` reads D99's Markdown with front matter as the format (id and title required, `apps`, `tags`, `explains`, `language`, `source-hash` for translations), and 11 topics are shipped in English and German (computes 5, composes 3, creates 1, typesetting 1, render 1). Still not done: links to related topics and to what a topic explains as checked links, an author guide, staleness of a topic against its subject.
