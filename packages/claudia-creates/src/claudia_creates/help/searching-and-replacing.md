**Edit ▸ Search & Replace…** (Ctrl+F) opens a panel over the slide. Type what you are looking for and the matches on
the slide are marked as you type; Enter goes to the next match, Shift+Enter to the previous one, and a match on another
slide takes you to that slide. Ctrl+H opens the same panel with **Replace with** already showing.

## Where it looks

The **Look in** button says what is searched: **Everything (4)**, or the names of the kinds that are on. Press it
for a list with a checkbox for each kind and the number of matches it has: **Text** (words in text boxes), **Shape
labels**, **Speaker notes** and **Descriptions** (the descriptions that are read aloud for an object). **All** and
**None** at the bottom of the list switch every kind on or off. The list beside the button says how far to look:
**All slides** or **This slide**; the checkboxes **Match case** and **Whole text** narrow it further. A match in the speaker notes shows the notes
under the slide.

## Moving the panel

Drag the panel by its title bar to put it where it does not cover what you are working on. It stays where you leave it
until you reload the page. From the keyboard, **Alt+arrow keys** move it (**Alt+Shift+arrow keys** in bigger steps;
Option on a Mac), unless the cursor is in a text field, where those keys keep their own meaning. Every dialog moves the
same way.

## Describing more than a word

**Build a pattern…** puts a search together from parts — "a word", "a letter, then one or more digits" — and a
sentence under them says back what is being found. **Only in texts that…** adds a demand on the whole text. Texts that
match the parts but fail a demand are listed under **Left out**, with the reason.

## Replacing

Switch on **Replace with…** and each match shows what it would become. Untick the ones to keep, then press
**Replace this one** or **Replace all**. All of it is one step in the history, so Undo brings every text back. An
object that someone else has selected, or whose words were changed after you looked, is left as it is and named.
